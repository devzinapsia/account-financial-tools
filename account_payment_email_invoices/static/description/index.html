Facturas adjuntas al enviar órdenes de pago y recibos

Payment orders and receipts sent by email carry the PDF of their invoices

When a payment order or a receipt is sent by email, the PDF of the invoices and credit notes that intervene in the payment is attached too, after the payment receipt and the withholding certificates, which stay exactly as before.

Key Features

  • Per company: one setting for payment orders, one for receipts, and journals whose documents are never attached (e.g. Go Live adjustments).
  • The right documents: invoices reconciled with the payment and credit notes applied within it; partial payments and invoices paid by several payments handled.
  • The right PDF: the same PDF the customer receives for sales documents, the vendor's PDF for bills; nothing is stored on the invoices.
  • Visible before sending: the documents show up in the composer and can be removed; a warning shows when the email exceeds the mail server size limit.
  • Every flow: single and mass sending, automatic sending with a template, scheduled messages.
