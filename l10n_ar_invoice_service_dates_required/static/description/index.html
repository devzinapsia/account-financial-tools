L10n AR Invoice Service Dates Required

Enforce ARCA service billing period dates on electronic sales journals

ARCA (formerly AFIP) requires the service billing period to be informed on Argentinian invoices whose concept is Services or Products and Services. This module makes the existing l10n_ar service period fields mandatory, both in the form view and at the data level, on electronic sales journals configured to enforce it.

Key Features

  • Per-journal validation: Enable or disable the requirement on each electronic sales journal.
  • Visual and data-level enforcement: The Service Date fields become required in the form and on save.
  • Scoped to the right invoices: Only applies to customer invoices/credit notes with a Services or Products and Services concept.
  • Single source of truth: One shared method drives both the view requirement and the create/write validation.
