Account Payment Authorization

Require another user's approval before confirming a vendor payment

Define configurable authorization schemes based on the vendor bill's classification, vendor, payment method, or amount. When a vendor payment matches a scheme, it stays pending until one of the scheme's authorized users approves or rejects it, right from an activity in their own inbox.

Key Features

  • Configurable schemes: combine classification, vendor, payment method and minimum amount conditions.
  • Multiple schemes, union of approvers: if several schemes match, any authorized user from any of them can approve.
  • Activity-based notifications: authorized users get an activity in "My Activities" and an email per their own preferences.
  • Server-side enforcement: only actual authorized users can approve or reject, never just a hidden button.
  • Traceable: chatter messages and a stored rejection reason keep a full audit trail.
