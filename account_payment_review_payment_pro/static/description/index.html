Revisión de órdenes de pago - Payment Pro

Compare the review amount against the whole payment order

Glue between account_payment_review and ingadhoc's account_payment_pro. Installs automatically when both are installed. Under account_payment_pro the amount that matters for a payment order is its total (payment_total): the payment itself plus write-off and, with l10n_ar_tax, withholdings. This module makes the Review from amount setting compare against that total, converted to company currency, instead of the payment's own amount. It also shows that total in the review wizard.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
