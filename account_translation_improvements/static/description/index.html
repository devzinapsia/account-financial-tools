Mejoras a traducciones contables

Collect / Collections on the customer side, Credit Notes on the vendor side

Odoo uses the same wording for customers and vendors in several places of the Invoicing app, which reads confusingly in Spanish: customer invoices show a "Pagar" button and the Customers menu has a "Pagos" entry, exactly like vendors. This module makes the customer side read "Cobrar" / "Cobros", and renames the vendor "Reembolsos" menu to "Notas de crédito".

Key Features

  • Invoice form: customer invoices, credit notes and receipts show a "Cobrar" button instead of "Pagar"; vendor documents keep "Pagar".
  • Customer lists: the header button of the customer invoice and credit note lists reads "Cobrar".
  • Gear menu: the invoice form's payment action reads "Pagar / Cobrar".
  • Menus: Clientes ‣ Cobros ("Cobros de clientes") and Proveedores ‣ Notas de crédito.
  • Clean uninstall: the original names and translations are restored.
