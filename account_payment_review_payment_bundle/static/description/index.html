Revisión de órdenes de pago - Pagos múltiples

Review the bundle's main payment, not each payment method

Glue between account_payment_review and ingadhoc's l10n_ar_payment_bundle. Installs automatically when both are installed. A payment bundle is made of a main payment (the payment order the user sees, with an amount of 0) and linked payments, one per payment method (cash, check, transfer...). This module makes only the main payment reviewable: linked payments stay out of the review circuit, its filters, the *Payments to review* menu and the review wizard. Together with account_payment_review_payment_pro, the Review from amount setting is compared against the bundle's total.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
