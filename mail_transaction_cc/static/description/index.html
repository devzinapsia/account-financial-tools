Correos en copia en transacciones

Real Cc and Bcc on the emails of invoices, receipts, payment orders, sales and purchases

Odoo sends one individual email per recipient, so adding partners or followers never produces a real copy. This module sends the customer or vendor a single email with your internal addresses in visible copy (Cc), plus an optional hidden copy (Bcc), configured by rules per transaction type and company.

Key Features

  • One real email: "To: customer" and "Cc: internal addresses" in the actual header; Bcc only in the SMTP envelope.
  • Five transaction types: customer invoices (credit and debit notes included), receipts, payment orders, quotations / sales orders and purchases.
  • Sales and Purchase without dependency: available as soon as each application is installed, without updating this module.
  • Visible and editable before sending: the sending wizards show the Cc and Bcc from the rules, and the user can change them for that sending.
  • Flexible recipients: free emails, users, groups resolved at sending time, and the document's responsible.
  • Two scopes: document sending only, or all communication including forwarding of incoming emails.
  • Safe by design: never on notes, automatic messages or other models; invalid rules are ignored instead of blocking the sending.
  • Works with receiptbooks: compatible with Adhoc's receiptbooks without depending on them.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
