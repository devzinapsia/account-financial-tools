Aviso de percepciones solo en ventas

Hide the perceptions fiscal position warning on vendor bills

The l10n_ar_tax module shows a warning when the invoice's fiscal position has perception taxes. Fiscal positions are detected by contact, so the warning also appears on vendor bills, where perceptions are never computed and the message is easily confused with the perceptions charged by the supplier. This module keeps the warning only on sale documents.

Key Features

  • Sales only: The warning is shown on customer invoices, credit notes and sales receipts.
  • No behavior change: Fiscal position detection and perception computation are untouched.
  • No configuration: Works right after installing.
