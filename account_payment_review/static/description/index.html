Revisión de órdenes de pago

Post-confirmation internal review of vendor payments

A reviewer goes through vendor payments that were already confirmed, one by one or in batches, and either signs them off as reviewed or records observations. The user who issued the payment gets an activity, answers the observations, and the payment goes back to the reviewer. The review never blocks nor modifies the payment or its journal entry.

Key Features

  • Review status badge: pending, reviewed, with observations or answered, next to the payment number.
  • Batch review: one wizard line per selected payment, each with its own result and comment.
  • Signed chatter messages: who reviewed, when, the result and the comment, as internal messages.
  • Observation loop: activities for the issuer and back for the reviewer, with a configurable deadline.
  • Exemptions: skip payments below an amount or matching custom conditions, from now on only.
  • Server-side permissions: only reviewers can review, never their own payments.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
