Transferencias intercompany

Move money between independent companies of the same database

A wizard transfers money from a bank or cash journal of one company to a bank or cash journal of another company. It posts, in one single transaction, an outgoing payment in the origin company and an incoming payment in the destination company, each one balanced against its own company's bridge account, with the other company as contact.

Key Features

  • One bridge account per company: a setting of its own, apart from Odoo's internal transfer account.
  • Atomic: both payments are created and posted together, or nothing is created.
  • No double count: the bank account only moves when each payment is reconciled with its statement line.
  • Balances by partner: the bridge account ledger grouped by partner shows the position with each company.
  • Own numbering per company: ICT/year/number, drawn by the origin company.
  • Safe cancellation: both payments at once, blocked if reconciled with a statement or in a closed period.
  • Server-side permissions: a dedicated access right, no superuser shortcuts, both companies required.
  • Kept apart from internal transfers: never mixed with Odoo's or ingadhoc's internal transfers.
