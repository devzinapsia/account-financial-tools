Go to **Invoicing / Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Invoicing ‣ Agreed payment
methods** to review, add, or edit the available agreed payment methods.

Each record has:

- **Name** (required, translatable)
- **Sequence** (controls display order)
- **Active** (uncheck to archive a method without deleting it)

Seven methods are loaded by default: Transferencia, Mercado Pago, Cheque,
Efectivo, Tarjeta precargada, Tarjeta de crédito, and Otro.

Only users with the *Accounting Manager* group can create, edit, or delete
agreed payment methods. Users with at least the *Billing* group can read
them and assign them to vendor bills.
