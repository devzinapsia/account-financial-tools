Mejoras a traducciones contables - Pagos por lotes

Batch Collections on the customer side

Glue module between account_translation_improvements and account_batch_payment, installed automatically. The customer batch payments menu and its screen title read "Cobros por lotes" instead of "Pagos por lotes", in line with the "Cobros" menu.

Key Features

  • Customer side: Clientes ‣ Cobros por lotes, and the same screen title.
  • Vendor side untouched: Proveedores ‣ Pagos por lotes keeps its name.
  • Clean uninstall: the original names and translations are restored.
  • Automatic: installed as soon as both modules are installed, no configuration.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
