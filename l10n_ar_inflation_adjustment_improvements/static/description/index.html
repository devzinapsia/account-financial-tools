Mejoras al ajuste por inflación

Exclude a whole closing/opening journal from the inflation adjustment

Adhoc's l10n_ar_account_reports inflation adjustment wizard can only exclude one closing entry and one opening entry from the calculation. This module adds the option to exclude every entry posted in a given miscellaneous journal instead, for companies that split their closing/opening into several entries.

Key Features

  • New question: "Specify individual entries?", shown when closing/opening entries were made.
  • Whole-journal exclusion: every entry of the selected journal is left out of both the initial balance and each monthly period.
  • Safe validation: the journal is required in Python too, and can't be the adjustment entry's own journal.
  • Translation fix: the wizard's "Journal" field now shows as "Diario".
